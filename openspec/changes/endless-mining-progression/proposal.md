## Why

The game needs a satisfying, open-ended mining loop that gives players ongoing reasons to explore, sell finds, improve skills, and upgrade pickaxes. A mine that scales beyond authored content while preserving player choice of familiar or deeper challenges supports relaxed, ongoing progression rather than a finite checklist.

## What Changes

- Introduce a mining progression experience centered on a base NPC service that buys found items, sells pickaxe upgrades at a premium, and offers focused mining-skill training.
- Provide a mine that expands sideways and downward without a finite content boundary; sideways exploration preserves each floor's enemy, item-value, and ore/pickaxe requirement bands, while deeper floors scale those bands.
- Gate ore mining by pickaxe tier and provide two non-circular upgrade routes: collecting accessible materials, or paying currency approximately twice those materials' currency-equivalent sale value.
- Support several specific mining-related skills that improve through use and can also receive focused training.
- Include approachable enemies whose every successful defeat makes measurable character-progression progress, even without a special drop.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `mining-progression`: Endless mine exploration, floor-based challenge and reward invariants, ore and pickaxe progression, base NPC services, skills, and approachable combat.

### Modified Capabilities

## Impact

This is a greenfield game capability affecting mine progression/content, resource and pickaxe systems, item selling, skills, enemy encounters, and base NPC services. No existing implementation, API, or dependencies were present to target during planning.
